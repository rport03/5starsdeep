Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

"5starsdeep poster" is a private publishing tool used only by the 5starsdeep channel to upload its own videos to its own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. It is not offered to the public and no one else signs in to it.

What it accesses

  • Only the 5starsdeep accounts that the channel owner connects, through each platform's official sign-in.
  • The permissions needed to upload videos with a title and caption, and to read the basic account details needed to upload (account and page IDs).

What it stores

Sign-in tokens issued by each platform are stored on the channel owner's own computer and are used only to upload the channel's videos. They are not sold, shared, or sent anywhere except back to the platform that issued them.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, read or store data about viewers, followers or other users.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties.

Deleting data

Disconnecting the app in a platform's settings revokes its access. The channel owner can delete the stored tokens at any time. Questions or data requests: 5starsdeephq@gmail.com.
